## 5. Energy

Method: `nvmlDeviceGetTotalEnergyConsumption, two reads per window, no sampling`. Idle draw measured at 3.215 W before the sweep and 1.913 W after; the spread between those two is the uncertainty on every above-idle figure below.

| kernel | mean W | mJ per call | pJ per weight byte |
|---|---|---|---|
| `q8_v0_naive` | 23.83 | 66.156 | 1381 |
| `q8_v1_warp` | 26.86 | 56.567 | 1181 |
| `q8_v2_smem_x` | 32.58 | 26.226 | 547 |
| `q8_v3_soa_vec` | 28.95 | 32.159 | 671 |
| `q8_v5_soa_smem` | 28.05 | 13.679 | 286 |
| `q4_v0_naive` | 23.53 | 37.047 | 1461 |
| `q4_v2_smem_x` | 22.74 | 18.191 | 717 |
| `q4_v5_soa_smem` | 22.72 | 16.263 | 641 |

From the naive kernel to the best one, mean power rose from 23.8 W to 28.1 W while energy per call fell 4.84x. The faster kernel draws more and costs less, which is the whole argument for racing to idle.

q4_0 costs 1.19x the energy per call of q8_0 and 2.25x the energy per byte moved. The format that reads fewer bytes is the more expensive one on both axes.
